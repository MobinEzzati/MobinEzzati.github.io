About

I'm an AI/ML engineer and builder based in Plano, Texas, finishing my MS in Computer Science with an AI/ML specialization at SMU (May 2026). I'm also AWS Certified as a Cloud Practitioner.

As co-founder and CTO of InfraOS, I work at the intersection of applied AI and real infrastructure — building tools that let large language models reliably act on enterprise systems, not just talk about them.

My background spans graduate NLP research, production MCP server development, and full-stack delivery — from FastAPI backends to AWS deployment with Terraform and Docker.
